Natural Language Processing, commonly known as NLP, is an important field of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to understand, process, analyze, and generate human language. 
Human language contains many words, expressions, meanings, and contexts, so understanding it can be a complex task for computers. 
NLP uses techniques from linguistics, computer science, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning to process text and speech. 
Common NLP applications include chatbots, language translation, sentiment analysis, speech recognition, text classification, question answering, and information extraction. 
Before processing text, systems may perform operations such as tokenization, removing unnecessary words, and converting words into useful numerical representations. 
Modern NLP systems can learn relationships between words and sentences from large collections of text. 
Search engines, virtual assistants, customer support applications, and document analysis systems use NLP technologies. 
The main objective of NLP is to make communication between humans and computers more natural, efficient, and meaningful.
