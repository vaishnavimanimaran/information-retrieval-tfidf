The Internet of Things, commonly known as IoT, is a technology that connects physical devices to the internet so that they can collect, exchange, and process data. 
IoT devices may contain sensors, processors, communication modules, and software that allow them to communicate with other devices or systems. 
Examples of IoT applications include smart watches, smart home appliances, connected vehicles, industrial machines, smart agriculture systems, and environmental monitoring devices. 
Sensors can collect information such as temperature, humidity, pressure, movement, location, and heart rate. 
This information can be transferred to edge devices or cloud platforms for further processing and analysis. 
IoT helps organizations improve automation, monitoring, resource management, and operational efficiency. 
However, security and privacy are important concerns because connected devices may become targets for cyber attacks. 
A successful IoT system requires reliable communication, suitable sensors, data processing, device management, and strong security measures to
protect the connected environment.
