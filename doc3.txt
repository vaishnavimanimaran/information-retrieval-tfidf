Deep Learning is a specialized branch of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.
These neural networks are inspired by the basic structure of the human brain and contain interconnected units called neurons. 
Deep Learning can automatically learn important features from raw data, which makes it useful for difficult tasks such as image recognition, speech recognition, natural language processing, and video analysis. 
Convolutional Neural Networks are commonly used for image-related applications, while other neural network architectures can be used for language and sequential data. 
Deep Learning requires suitable training data, computational resources, and optimization techniques to develop accurate models. 
It is used in healthcare for medical image analysis, in transportation for autonomous vehicles, and in virtual assistants for speech understanding. 
As the amount and quality of training data increase, Deep Learning models can often achieve better performance on complex problems.
