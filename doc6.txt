Data Science is an interdisciplinary field that uses statistics, mathematics, programming, and domain knowledge to collect, process, analyze, and interpret data. 
The main purpose of Data Science is to discover useful patterns and information from data and use them to support better decisions. 
A data scientist usually performs several activities such as data collection, data cleaning, data preprocessing, exploratory analysis, visualization, feature selection, model development, and evaluation. 
Data can be structured, such as information stored in tables and databases, or unstructured, such as text, images, audio, and videos. 
Python is widely used in Data Science because libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Scikit-learn provide useful tools for data analysis and visualization. 
Data Science is applied in healthcare, banking, marketing, education, business, and scientific research. 
Good quality data is important because incorrect or incomplete data can produce unreliable results. 
Data Science helps organizations understand information and make accurate predictions.
