Cloud Computing is a technology that provides computing resources such as servers, storage, databases, networking, and software through the internet. 
Instead of purchasing and maintaining large amounts of physical hardware, organizations can use cloud services according to their requirements. 
Cloud Computing provides flexibility because users can increase or decrease resources when needed. The three major cloud service models 
are Infrastructure as a Service, Platform as a Service, and Software as a Service. Cloud environments can also be deployed as public, private, or hybrid clouds 
depending on the needs of an organization. Important benefits of Cloud Computing include scalability, remote access, high availability, faster development, and reduced infrastructure maintenance. 
Cloud platforms are used for storing data, hosting websites, developing applications, performing business analysis, and running Artificial Intelligence systems. 
However, organizations must consider security, privacy, cost management, and reliable internet connectivity when using cloud services. 
Cloud Computing has become an important part of modern digital infrastructure.
