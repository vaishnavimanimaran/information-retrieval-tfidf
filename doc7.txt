Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, software applications, and digital information from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, and disruption.
As people and organizations depend more on digital technologies, protecting information has become very important. 
Common cyber threats include phishing, malware, ransomware, password attacks, social engineering, and denial of service attacks. 
Cyber security professionals use several methods to reduce these risks, including firewalls, encryption, antivirus software, secure authentication, intrusion detection systems, and regular software updates. 
Strong passwords and multi-factor authentication can provide additional protection for user accounts. 
Organizations should also train employees to identify suspicious emails, links, and messages. 
Cyber security includes activities such as risk assessment, security monitoring, incident response, and data recovery. 
The three main goals of information security are confidentiality, integrity, and availability. 
A strong cyber security system helps protect sensitive information and ensures that important digital services remain safe and reliable.
